💡 Professional Recording Tips

Small details that make a big difference in video quality.

🖥️
1920×1080 Resolution
Set your display to 1080p before recording. OBS: set canvas to 1920×1080 in Settings → Video.
🔇
Mute Notifications
Enable Do Not Disturb mode (Windows: Focus Assist → Alarms Only) before recording starts.
🖱️
Slow Smooth Mouse
Move your cursor slowly and deliberately. Viewers need time to follow. Pause 1 second on each element.
🌐
Clean Browser
Use Incognito/Private mode so no bookmarks, extensions, or personal data appears on screen.
🎨
Dark Mode = Cinematic
Record in dark mode — it looks more professional and shows less browser chrome. Toggle in the navbar.
🔍
Zoom In on Details
Use Ctrl+Plus to zoom in when showing forms, charts, or numbers. Zoom out between scenes.
🎙️
Voice or Text
Record with a microphone OR add text captions in DaVinci Resolve. Either approach is professional.
📱
Show Mobile View
Press F12 → Toggle Device Mode in Chrome → show mobile responsiveness. Great ending shot!
